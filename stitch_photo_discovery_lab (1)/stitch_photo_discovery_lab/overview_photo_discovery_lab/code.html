1. Ingestion Filter

Posts are parsed via Google Photos Community connectors using query patterns targeting failure terms (e.g., "search can't find", "cannot retrieve", "zero results", "wrong date").

2. Taxonomic Triage

Zero-shot classification categorizes posts into Semantic-Object, Spatial-Vague, Temporal-Drift, Face-Clustering, or System-Index categories.

3. Pipeline Gate

Automated LLM pipeline verifies that the verbatim reflects genuine photo retrieval struggle rather than generic account access issues.
