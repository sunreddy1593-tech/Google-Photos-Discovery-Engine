Ethics & Governance

Provenance & Privacy Guarantees

public

Public-Only Provenance

All text originates exclusively from open, indexable web forums—Google Support Community, Reddit (r/googlephotos), YouTube help threads, and publicly posted App Store feedback. No private channels, private issue trackers, or closed customer portals were crawled.

check_circle Zero Non-Public Scraping
no_photography

Zero Personal Photo Access

Photo Discovery Lab operates strictly on qualitative textual expressions of search behavior. The system contains no API bindings, credentials, or architectural bridges to user photo albums, Google Drive assets, image metadata, or account storage containers.

shield Zero Account Ingestion
badge

PII Sanitization & Redaction

Every user verbatim passes through a strict automated entity scrubber before entering the workbench. Real usernames, emails, phone numbers, and location coordinates are wiped and converted into anonymized research tokens such as User_GP_482.

Methodological Boundaries • Essential Evaluator Context

Research Limitations & Corpus Guardrails

While this workbench provides sharp qualitative insights into user retrieval breakdown, findings must be evaluated alongside known methodological constraints:

pie_chart Public Forum Sampling Bias

Voluntary forum posters naturally skew heavily toward severe friction, confusion, or vocal discontent. Seamless, instantaneous one-shot photo searches are rarely posted to Reddit or help forums, meaning this corpus measures failure states rather than overall product satisfaction.

psychology Self-Reported Memory Decay

Verbatims capture the user's retrospective recollection of their search query, not an instrumented telemetry keystroke log. Users frequently simplify or omit intermediate attempts when recounting multi-stage retrieval workflows in open discussion threads.

inventory Illustrative Sample Scope

All calculated quality metrics and cluster distributions reflect this specific 1,420-document evaluated corpus. Statistical proportions should be viewed as illustrative exploratory findings rather than canonical corporate metrics for Google Photos user behavior at global scale.
